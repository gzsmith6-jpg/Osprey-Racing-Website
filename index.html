Dynamic Events

Endurance/Fuel Economy

Tests the overall performance of the car and its reliability. The car must drive 22 km with a mandatory driver change, and its fuel consumption is measured.

Skid Pad

Measures the car’s cornering ability while making a constant-radius turn. Drivers make laps around a small figure-eight course.

Acceleration

Cars accelerate over a distance of 75 m on a flat surface.

Autocross

Evaluates the car’s maneuverability and handling qualities on a tight course without competing cars. The course combines acceleration, braking, and cornering.

Static Events

Business Presentation

Evaluates the team’s ability to develop and deliver a comprehensive business plan. The car must be profitably manufactured, marketed, and meet the demands of the racing market.

Engineering Design

Judges the effort that went into the car’s design. The car that best uses engineering to meet its design goals will win the design event.

Cost Analysis

Cost and budget are significant factors. Teams must make decisions between cost and benefit, and create and maintain a Bill of Material.

About Osprey Racing

Osprey Racing is a non-profit Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE) at the University of North Florida dedicated to designing, building, and racing a formula-style racecar annually.

Founded in January 2010, the program has grown into one of the most recognized and respected organizations on campus, relying entirely on community sponsors for financial, material, and technical resources.

Building upon a rich history of hands-on engineering excellence highlighted by the record-breaking Swoop D12 at the 2024 Michigan competition and two trophies at the Daytona International Speedway SCCA autocross, Osprey Racing continues to push the boundaries of student-built high-performance motorsport.

Biography

Dr. John Nuszkowski is an Associate Professor at the University of North Florida. His research goals are the improvement of vehicle fuel economy by operating vehicles more efficiently and the improvement of air quality by reducing the exhaust emissions from engines and vehicles through improved combustion of petroleum-based fuels and use of alternative fuels. Dr. Nuszkowski’s research includes vehicle powertrain optimization, in-use vehicle emissions, advanced combustion, alternative fuels, and large bore diesel emissions reduction.
